Aqua Jar

Complete product guide — every screen, every button, every flow

Aqua Jar is a phone-first accounts app for packaged drinking-water plants that deliver 20-litre jars to shops. It replaces the paper register: the delivery phone taps numbers on the route; the plant dashboard sees the same numbers live; bills and a statement come out of those numbers.

This document is for anyone who needs the full picture — product, operations, and UI — including an AI assistant reviewing the project. Button names below are the actual in-app labels (default language: Hindi written in Roman letters, e.g. Key banao).

Product: Aqua Jar · Delivery
Audience: plant owner and delivery staff
Stack: browser app (HTML/CSS/JS) + Supabase
Document date: 9 September 2026

1. What this app is

One plant = one company account. One or more delivery people, each with a phone. Every filled jar that leaves the plant, every jar given to a shop, every empty collected, every leak, every broken jar, and every rupee of udhari is one shared ledger.

Simple by design. Almost no typing on the route. Plus and minus. Two numbers per shop: how many filled jars given (Diye) and how many empties collected (Khali uthe). Then Delivery pura ✓. Plant side is mostly reading numbers and tapping a customer to open a bill.
Universal by design. Not built only for one named shop in Jalgaon. Any 20L jar plant can sign up: unique username, firm name on the invoice, own delivery staff, own shops, own rates. Five languages: Hindi (Roman), हिंदी, English, ગુજરાતી, मराठी. Works in the phone browser; login stays on that phone until Logout.

2. Why plants need this (problems the app actually solves)

  1. Writing given / empty in a notebook at each shop is slow and error-prone. Phone: + / −.
  2. Nobody knows which shop still has how many jars to pick up. App: live market list per shop.
  3. Making a monthly bill takes time. App: tap customer → invoice (print or save image).
  4. Jars and rupees get mixed in one copy. App: market vs udhari are separate.
  5. Plant learns the day’s numbers only when the boy returns. App: live dashboard.
  6. Load from plant vs jars given do not match. App: confirm load; counted vs expected at return.
  7. Leak and breakage treated as the same loss. App: leak = empty jar exists; toot = jar gone.
  8. Route order forgotten. App: sequence (arrows + drag).
  9. Month register to total by date. App: month table, diye on top / khali below / dates across.
  10. Who paid, who still owes. App: rate, payment, old pending, net udhari, balance sheet.
  11. Several delivery men. App: each has a name, key, and own shops.
  12. Plant password should not go to the boy. App: username + key only.
  13. Yesterday’s figure was wrong. Delivery can edit today + yesterday + 2 days ago only.

3. How simple it is (interaction model)

  • Almost no keyboard on the route. Steppers (− number +). Number can also be typed.
  • Big primary buttons for the one next action: Confirm — gadi bhari, Delivery pura ✓, Login.
  • Bottom tabs only. Plant: Dashboard / Mahina / Market / Hisaab. Delivery: Route / Customers / + Naya.
  • One header everywhere: firm name, Help, Logout, date, Live (plant) or remaining-time chip (delivery today).
  • Speaker next to shop names and help text — useful when hands are wet or reading is slow.
  • Language picker on login and in the header. Changing language relabels the whole UI.
  • Help overlay on first plant login (welcome → fayda → who delivers → key → share → login → where Help is). Reopen anytime.
  • Copy key shows a 3-second tick. No alert popup.

14. Delivery man — full daily flow

  1. Login: Delivery man tab → company username + key.
  2. Add shops if needed: + Naya customer (name + optional place). No rate on this phone — plant sets ₹.
  3. Enter how many filled jars left the plant. Confirm — gadi bhari. Until this, the load box stays at the top; after confirm, customers move up.
  4. Walk remaining shops. Per shop: Diye, Khali uthe, see “iske baad market mein baki”, then Delivery pura ✓.
  5. Optional: rokda (cash sold), sequence of shops.
  6. When all shops are pura: day summary moves up. Fill Plant par wapas: leak, toot, total jars counted back on the vehicle.
  7. If counted ≠ expected, warning to recount. Match = hisaab match.

16. Rules the software enforces

RuleDetail
Market ≠ udhariJars at shops never equal rupees due.
Leak ≠ tootLeak keeps the jar; toot removes it.
Load confirmRoute numbers make sense only after filled-out is confirmed.
Stock checkGiven + leak/toot cannot exceed load from plant (warning).
3-day delivery editToday, yesterday, 2 days ago. Older blocked in UI and database (RLS).
Plant vs delivery loginPassword never used on delivery phone. Key is not the plant password.
UsernameLowercase, no spaces; 3+; unique per plant.
Key brute-force4 failures → wait.
SessionCookie/localStorage; one phone stays in until Logout.
RateBills use current rate. Note on sheet: old bills can change if rate is edited (E. & O.E.).
Cash in bankNot tracked. Sheet says cash/bank is not in the app.

17. What is not in the app (on purpose)

  • No GPS tracking of the vehicle.
  • No WhatsApp sending from inside the app (you copy the key and send yourself).
  • No GST fields / HSN on the invoice (simple trade invoice for 20L jar).
  • No multi-product SKU (only 20L jar).
  • No staff attendance, salary, or diesel.
  • No customer-facing app for shops.

18. Technical picture (for a reviewer)

  • Client: static files under app/ — index.html, css/app.css, ES modules (app.js, store.js, db.js, auth.js, i18n.js, help.js).
  • Local server: npm run dev → typically http://localhost:5173/.
  • Backend: Supabase (Postgres + Auth-style org rows + RLS). Tables prefixed sa_. Full schema: supabase/sanjay_aqua_full.sql. Extra: supabase/driver_edit_3days.sql.
  • Live: plant dashboard subscribes so new deliveries appear without refresh.
  • PWA-ish: manifest, theme colour, apple web-app title Aqua Jar.
  • Print/PNG: CSS print hide chrome; html2canvas for save image.
Do not put the Supabase service_role key in the app. Only the anon public key. Do not touch database tables that are not sa_*.

19. End-to-end story (one plant, one boy)

  1. Owner: Naya account → username kkkk, firm name, password.
  2. Help overlay: welcome, fayde, “maine delivery man lagaya hai”.
  3. + Add → “Mayur” → Key banao → Copy.
  4. WhatsApp Mayur: company username (header) + key.
  5. Mayur: login Delivery man, add “Akash gym”, confirm 20 jars on the gadi.
  6. Shop: Diye 2, Khali 1, Delivery pura.
  7. Owner phone: dashboard fills; Market shows leftover jars; Live is green.
  8. Mayur finishes route, counts return; mismatch would flag.
  9. Owner sets rate on Akash gym, opens bill, prints, records payment.
  10. Mahina / Hisaab for the accountant or the owner’s own close.
